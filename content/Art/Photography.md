My relationship with photography is very intertwined with [[Hyperart Thomasson|hyperart]], as the latter is what got me into it in the first place. It initally began as a way to hunt and record [[Hyperart Thomasson|hyperart]] I found in my day to day life, but quickly became a passion in of itself. 

Photography encourages me to pay more attention to the little details in my surroundings. It takes the passivity out of existing in transitory spaces and instead has you far more mindful and aware. I get a great amount of joy from the littlest things, even if I don't necessarily take a picture Im still trained to notice them.
![[P1040669.jpg]]It as an art form is also very rewarding to improve at. There's something special about seeing your environment turn into something so artistic with the right angle. 

I find a lot of merit in the physicality of a camera. I prefer to be decentralised from my phone (frankly if it were at all practical I would rather not have one) and so to have a device dedicated to photos does a lot for that.
Equally it keeps all of my more meaningful pictures in one place. Rather than sifting through a camera roll full of more benign or function-focused pictures (or just random shit Ive saved from online), I know all of the pictures I care about are in one place; a place that is also easier to transfer to an external hard drive for safekeeping.  

My camera isnt anything fancy, an old 2006 lumix I got second hand for cheap, and Ive grown to love it deeply. Its hardly the most capable, but if used right can take fantastic pictures, and is easily portable. There isnt much I would change about it.
