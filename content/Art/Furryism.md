I have a particular relationship with the furry label. 
The identity is a very broad one and for which there is no requirement. It can be as complicated as a life philosophy, or as simple as liking a character design, however you choose to apply it to yourself is all it needs to be to you. 
For me its a represntation of an individuals adversity to normality. 

Nobody is completely normal, if there was absolutely nothing unusual about someone that in itself would make them an outlier. It is not unique to be unique, to not completely fit in societally; normality is a fluid and hazy construct of unspoken rules and paths of least resistance, it is not designed for anyone in particular.
I find depicting myself as an anthropomorphic animal very apt for how I interact with "normality". To be human like, but obviously not human: my shape, my faculties, my abilities, all beget me to engage in the functions of society, yet so many parts of me are at odds with it. Huge limbs, unwieldy hands, strange ears and a huge fucking tail; life in the real world would be full of inconveniences, maybe even borderline impossible. 
Yet my nature would equally permit me to do so many things I couldn't otherwise: to swim freely, have the strength and flexibility for great feats, heightened senses, etc. It's a give and take relationship, not inherently a downgrade but instead determined by how my environment allows me to express and engage with my traits. In thats sense, it's identity made manifest.

Being neurodivergent means I excel in some ways and struggle in others, ways my environment was not built to accommodate nor cope with. The physical clashes my nonstandard body has with its surroundings is representative of the invisible ones I as an individual have with life at large. 
The expression of those difficulties is catharsis enough, but to as well be proud of them is an even greater form of love. I exaggerate those animalistic features because I love who I am, to be depicted as feral and loud is a celebration of the self, not because its perfect or because its who I am but because its true to my experience of life, frictive yet freeing.
