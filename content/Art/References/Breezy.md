Their design is very malleable, with a variety of their features being either toggleable or being up to artistic exxageration. Though the exact details of this are below. 

**Design**
- The spike fins across their body are located at their:
	- Shoulders
	- Knees
	- Forearms (toggleable)
	- Back middle of the tail near the base 
	- Two on the bottom left and bottom right of the tail near the base
	- Two on the left and right of the tail near the tip 
	- Two on either side of the hips (toggleable)
	- Down the centre of their back (toggleable)
- Their big forearm fins can either be extended or retracted. When retracted, they come to be little points on the elbows. 
- Their anatomy is very trunky, I enjoy when their arms and legs are exaggerated into big club-like appendages. Inspirations for this include the werehog, ori and tigress / tai lung from kung fu panda. 
- They can be digitigrade or plantigrade, though I personally find plantigrade sells exaggerated anatomy better. 
- Their pupils are three vertical black slits within a white / light-grey iris
