<font style="color:grey"> They / It | Lowercase name only </font>
Without species, aalto is a strange amalgamation.  
They are defined by their difficulties surrounding communication, representation and connection. They never learnt, nor have much capacity or want, for condensive mediums. To think of the world through arbitrary or contrived concepts is in many ways beyond them, and instead it engages with the world as raw and as pure as their singular perspective can allow. 
This is not born out of some underlying philosophy or goal, but rather is a result of their long solitary existence and how they best feel comfortable. They are a pure sense of self, representative of the personal superposition of identity, and the pain of its collapse under observation despite it being necessary for connection.

Untouched, unseen, and alone.

I have struggled with their design a lot and so a lot of things change, but there are some consistent features.

**Design**
- Stick legs, bendable but coming to an asbtract point; no feet 
	Can be digitigrade or plantigrade 
- Siamese patterning growing darker down the legs 
- Winged hands made of three large almost geometric feathers 
- A pair of wings on the back of a similar geometric style
	Placement is free to decide 
- A pair of small wings on the head 
- Cat like tail
- Three eyes on one side of the head 
	Can be either side 
- No mouth
- Patterns are non exact and are inspired by damascus steel 
	Changes to contrast whatever colour theyre on, eg black when on white fur but white when on black fur 

**Behaviour**
- They are representative of the pure sense of self than can only exist within the person, as any translation of it into a communicable medium loses or changes some aspect of it, and as such have a great fear around communication and disdain for any amount of projection onto the world. To them, nothing deserves that misrepresentation. 
- They prefer not to speak, but if they do they do so in [[toki pona]] in sign language, of which I designed a system: [[toki waso]]

I don't use them for much anymore as they were designed to represent a very specific emotion that, after a lot of personal development, I no longer deal with as acutely.
I still love them greatly, but between my finnickiness with their design and their "function" kind of falling away, I struggle to include them much.
