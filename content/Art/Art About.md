#### <font style="color:grey"> olin wawa e musi kon tan seme? </font>
Art doesnt need explanation, its a form of an inherent human(?) desire to express yourself and connect with others. The fulfilment of the process, the exploration of what you put into it, the reach and commonality it can bring with others, its a lifeblood.
And yet equally, its not anything at all. Art is not an inherent trait, its a projected concept. Art is not art to those who do not perceive it as art, just as it is undeniably art to others who do take it as such.

It all starts and ends at your mind, so love what you do and love it loudly, nothing can tell you otherwise. 

<br>

#### <font style="color:grey"> mi pali anu moku e seme? </font> 
I touch bases with a variety of mediums for my own work. I've grown up drawing and writing for a large part of my life, and have overtime learnt to find art in hobbies like photography, [[Content About|video making]], cooking, baking, [[Tech About|technology]] and other small forms.
I am resolved to make a career out of creating in some form or another. I don't think I can live without it. 

As for what interests me I have a love for works of introspection and storytelling. I have a particular love of [[Music About|music]] as a self-exposee medium as I find the nature of sound a fantastically unconstrained medium for those more abstract concepts that change and morph in ugly ways under more traditional communications. 
Drawings are less experiential and more flashbulb to me, and I love them for more methodological reasons; that process of translation is so difficult yet so deliberate. 
My love of stories often stems from their tendency to lampshade other aspects of life, a microcosm that is moving and compelling in its own right. 
Games as a medium are often time bombastic culminations of all these characteristics and more, just as much constrained by their nature as they are enabled by it, theyre a fascinating tightrope to walk and so utterly experiential. The best expression of my love for the medium at the moment would be [this video of mine about ULTRAKILL](https://youtu.be/pRaELX6esTg).
