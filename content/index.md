# Hello! Welcome to my website
#### <font style="color:grey"> toki! kama pona lon mi lipu </font>  
![[Hello.png]]
<br> 
<font style="color:grey"> Art by [rikamori](https://vgen.co/rikaamori)</font>

I have never been a social media person, and as such have never built that muscle to "put yourself out there". I still hold no interest in social media, but as I begin to create and make for more people than just myself I realise I ought to have some material put up for those who wish to interact with me or my stuff. 

I find a personal website like this the best way to do so. I intend for it to largely be static and act as a reference point or information bulletin that I update as and when needed, but is otherwise unburdened by any sense of immediacy or attention/retention.
This site will contain a mixture of resources regarding my hobbies & work for those who may benefit from that, and more personal asides intended to paint a picture of who I am and what I do. This page here will act as a link-tree.
