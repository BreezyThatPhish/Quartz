# Main Rig 

My PC is decently beefy, no supercomputer but certainly strong. It serves me well for the vast majority of what I do, and has been an ongoing tinkering project since I built it in 2022 (of which was my first ever PC build, thank you Canaan). 
Maybe a little ship of Theseus at this point but what good computer isnt. 

- AMD Ryzen 9 5900X
- AMD Radeon RX 7800XT
- Corsair Vengeance 32GB DDR4
- MSI MPG B550 Gaming Plus 
- Samsung 970 Evo Plus 2TB x2
- Seagate Barracuda 6TB
- MSI MAG A850GL

I'm currently looking to build a second PC actually, a small form factor one that I'll use for a variety of odd jobs, notably to offload streaming encoding onto (among various data backup solutions). Looking forward to when I can update this page with that thing.

# Peripherals 

I have a variety of unorthodox peripherals that stem from a mixture of enthusiast dabbling and questions over ergonomics, and they're really what make my setup mine. 

I love hearing and seeing other peoples setups because this is where their identity can best be seen; the way they interface with their computer is like a fingerprint, revealing what they find important, what they dont, what they prioritise, what their interests are, so on. 
Its a little window into their world, like a mattress depression of personality.
