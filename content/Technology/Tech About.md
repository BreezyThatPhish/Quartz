#### <font style="color:grey"> olin e ilo tawa pali </font>
I am a big tech enthusiast, I love tinkering with hardware and prodding around at software. Mind you I'm no programmer, I can't speak a coding language to save my life, but I am very able and willing to fuck around and find out (repurposing java, tinkering C+, poking around at css, etc). 

Technology is a fantastic tool that enables you to do so many things, but is best when it as used as such. I have a strong dislike for technology that intrudes upon or dictates your life, as opposed to the other way around. All things considered I live a rather unplugged and offline life. 
I spend a lot of time with technology, but its in the places and at the times I choose. I would hate to be surrounded by "smart devices" or connected to some kind of bandwidth 24/7 (frankly I'm waiting for my current phone to die so I can invest in some kind of dummy phone). 

It's this particular relationship with technology that leads to me making the choices I do regarding what i utilise in my life, and why I have such a love of what I find.
