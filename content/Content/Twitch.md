#### <font style="color:grey"> mi lon tenpo ni tawa kulupu </font>

I've tried streaming a few times throughout my life, typically way way too early. I have many unlisted videos on my youtube of 14 year old me on the ps4 streaming for hours to nobody and barely saying anything. Its been incredibly cool to finally start making something out of it.

I find the strength of streaming as a medium to be the interactivity with others. My favourite parts about it is when chat influences and forms the stream just as much, if not more, than myself. Its an extension of community sure, but the mutual product is far greater than whatever could have been made by either party alone. It becomes an emergent quality. 
This is why I prefer to make stream highlights compared to recorded gameplay, the latter lacks that dynamic.

I've been watching stream highlight content for years, its the primary form of content that I personally consume on youtube. People like [RT](https://www.youtube.com/@RTGame), [Doug](https://www.youtube.com/@DougDoug) and [Carson](https://www.youtube.com/@CallMeCarsonLIVE) have all been foundational inspirations for this endeavour
The community and relationship that streaming builds with viewers that can then be turned into a fancy production over for youtube is a very fulfilling dynamic, both for viewer and for creator, and thus is the dynamic I have (in part) staked my content on.

I hope to one day build a community not too dissimilar to those named above.

Built entirely within OBS mostly using the [image reaction](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/image-reaction.1342/) plugin, I have a whole note explaining how I set it up [[PNGTuber|here]].

#### <font style="color:grey"> Streamavatars </font>
I use streamavatars for the little guys at the bottom of my setup. They get given to every viewer on the stream, but there are a ton of options that change their behaviour, allocation, interaction etc. 
Its on steam and has a huge community workshop full of custom avatars, and making your own isnt hard either.

#### <font style="color:grey"> Multistreaming </font>
I stream both to YT and Twitch simultaneously using [aitum multistream](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/aitum-multistream.1991/), a simple obs plugin that leverages your upload bandwidth to stream to multiple outputs at once.
This is not the only multistreaming solution but due to me having rather good internet its the one I personally chose.

For my multichat widget I use [nutty.gg's overlay](https://nutty.gg/en-gbp/products/multichat-overlay), and whilst it requires [streamerbot](https://streamer.bot/) to function, it's one of the more customisable and aesthetically pleasing overlays I have found.
