#### <font style="color:grey"> mi lon tenpo ni tawa kulupu </font>

I've tried streaming and youtuber-ing a few times throughout my life, typically way way too early. I have many unlisted videos on my youtube of 14 year old me on the ps4 streaming for hours to nobody and barely saying anything, or lackluster titanfall 2 compilations and highlight reels that got viewcounts you could quantify on your hands. It has been incredibly cool to finally start making something out of it, Ive already gotten farther than I ever though I would.

I find the strength of streaming as a medium to be the interactivity with others. My favourite parts about it is when chat influences and forms the stream just as much, if not more, than myself. Its an extension of community sure, but the mutual product is far greater than whatever could have been made by either party alone. It becomes an emergent quality. 
This is why I prefer to make stream highlights compared to recorded gameplay, the latter lacks that dynamic.

I've been watching stream highlight content for years, its the primary form of content that I personally consume on youtube. People like [RT](https://www.youtube.com/@RTGame), [Doug](https://www.youtube.com/@DougDoug) and [Carson](https://www.youtube.com/@CallMeCarsonLIVE) have all been foundational inspirations for this endeavour
The community and relationship that streaming builds with viewers that can then be turned into a fancy production over for youtube is a very fulfilling dynamic, both for viewer and for creator, and thus is the dynamic I have (in part) staked my content on.

I hope to one day build a community not too dissimilar to those named above.

There is a second half to my content enthusiasm, and that being more longform essayist work. 
Essays are a lot more effort, but there are few creative projects that I find more fulfilling.
To thoroughly explore a topic I hold close to my heart in a way that both incorporates my love of writing & theatrics and connects or speaks with others is an incredible endeavour.
Its a more drawn out enthusiasm, working on something thats end result so intently rests on the quality of each individual part and its connections to those others around it. I find having a place to sink so much passion fantastically rewarding. 

I've been writing stories and loving books since I was like 5, I have a distinct memory of performatively acting out authoring a book by sitting at the dining room table with some kind of hot drink and writing into a word document some banal story about something or other. It was the first time I ever used word. At that point I still found my Dads toothpaste too spicy.

The essayist format is something I try and incorporate into all my content to some degree due to my love of writing and theatric articulation, though obviously to differing degrees depending on the work. 
And whilst dedicated essay videos are fewer and further between than more traditional highlights due to their workload and scope, theyre some of the projects I look forward to the most.

#### <font style="color:grey"> PNGTuber </font>
Built entirely within OBS mostly using the [image reaction](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/image-reaction.1342/) plugin, I have a whole note explaining how I set it up [[PNGTuber|here]]. 
![[ezgif-3a845aeca74d7183.gif]]
Due to not utilising any external software, and its sprite & volume based activation, its an incredibly robust and flexible setup that allows you to make some really dynamic stuff. 
I would recommend it over more traditional pntuber softwares for certain usecases. 
<br>

#### <font style="color:grey"> Streamavatars </font>
I use [streamavatars](https://store.steampowered.com/app/665300/Stream_Avatars/) for the little guys at the bottom of my setup. They get given to every viewer on the stream, but there are a ton of options that change their behaviour, allocation, interaction etc. 
Its on steam and has a huge community workshop full of custom avatars, and making your own isnt hard either. 
![[Pasted image 20260928160920.png|800]]

<br>

#### <font style="color:grey"> Multistreaming </font>
I stream both to YT and Twitch simultaneously using [aitum multistream](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/aitum-multistream.1991/), a simple obs plugin that leverages your upload bandwidth to stream to multiple outputs at once.
This is not the only multistreaming solution but due to me having rather good internet its the one I personally chose.

For my multichat widget I use [nutty.gg's overlay](https://nutty.gg/en-gbp/products/multichat-overlay), and whilst it requires [streamerbot](https://streamer.bot/) to function, it's one of the more customisable and aesthetically pleasing overlays I have found. 

<br>

#### <font style="color:grey"> Stream Decks </font>
I have two stream decks, one is a [Stream Deck+](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/stream-deck-plus) and another [Stream Deck 15 Key](https://www.elgato.com/us/en/p/stream-deck), the latter of which was kindly gifted to me by a friend. 
The software I use for it is [Stream Controller](https://github.com/StreamController/StreamController), a piece of software I have many gripes with and frankly eats up far too much of my pc's resources, but works fairly well and allows me to do a good deal of tricks.
