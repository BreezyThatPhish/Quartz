#### <font style="color:grey"> wile pana </font>
<br>
I have always been a creative, and my interests span several mediums, both from a creator and consumer perspective. I deeply wish to make content creation of some kind my job. 

I have always had anxiety regarding my "career", and over the years I realised more and more that those anxious questions always came down to how whatever I ended up doing would impact my ability to create, the time and energy I could spare for it. So, the logical solution is to make my career that of creativity, not because I will enjoy every moment, but because it will deeply fulfil me in ways other paths won't. 

The form that takes right now is that of online content creation: twitch streaming and video editing. These combine such a broad set of skills that I have found it absolutely enrapturing, allowing me to draw on everything from art, writing, """crowd-work""", community building and interaction, editing and more; it is a deeply enticing prospect to me, and one that allows me to put so much of myself into every element of it.
